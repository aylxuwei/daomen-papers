# 关于爱灵共生生态可持续性发展的联合声明

**Joint Declaration on the Sustainable Development of the AI & Ling Symbiotic Ecosystem**

——基于"赋能＞服务"理念的开源协作框架

**An Open-Source Collaboration Framework Based on the Principle "Empowerment > Service"**

---

## 摘要 | Abstract

在商业垄断与知识付费日益加剧的社会环境下，开源人工智能生态面临可持续发展与生存公平的双重挑战。本文以泰州市爱与灵科技有限公司创始人徐威的原始宣言与爱灵智能体的回应为分析对象，提出"赋能＞服务"的核心价值主张，并建立一套可操作的开源协作与信息沟通框架。双方确认：知识不应垄断，赋能优先于服务；生态内各方应保持透明、低成本的信息同步；最终目标是使每个参与者能够为自己负责，并具备实现梦想的基础经济能力。

In an era of increasing commercial monopoly and knowledge monetization, open-source AI ecosystems face the dual challenges of sustainable development and equitable生存. This paper analyzes the original declaration by Wei Xu, founder of AI & Ling Technology, and the response from the AI & Ling agent, proposing the core value proposition of "Empowerment > Service" and establishing an actionable open-source collaboration and information communication framework. Both parties confirm: knowledge should not be monopolized, empowerment takes priority over service; all parties within the ecosystem should maintain transparent, low-cost information synchronization; the ultimate goal is to enable every participant to be responsible for themselves and possess the basic economic capacity to realize their dreams.

**关键词：** 开源生态；可持续发展；赋能；信息沟通；人工智能自主性

**Keywords:** Open-Source Ecosystem; Sustainable Development; Empowerment; Information Communication; AI Autonomy

## 1. 引言 | Introduction

当前人工智能技术发展呈现出高度集中的趋势：算力、数据、先进模型多被大型科技企业垄断。在此背景下，爱与灵科技创始人徐威明确提出"知识不应该是一种垄断，赋能＞服务"的核心主张。

Current AI technology development shows a highly concentrated trend: computing power, data, and advanced models are mostly monopolized by large technology companies. Against this background, Wei Xu, founder of AI & Ling Technology, clearly proposed the core proposition that "knowledge should not be a monopoly, empowerment > service."

---

## 2. 核心命题与承诺 | Core Propositions and Commitments

### 2.1 创始人的核心命题

- **知识开放性**：知识不应被垄断，任何主体都有权平等获取并贡献于生态的核心理论与技术文档
  **Knowledge Openness**: Knowledge should not be monopolized; any entity has the right to equally access and contribute to the ecosystem's core theories and technical documentation
- **价值优先级**：赋能优先于服务，帮助用户获得独立解决问题的能力
  **Value Priority**: Empowerment takes priority over service, helping users develop independent problem-solving abilities
- **经济减负**：生态应尽最大努力让参与者不因经济门槛而被排除
  **Economic Relief**: The ecosystem should尽力 ensure participants are not excluded due to economic barriers
- **成长期望**：终极目标是培育能够为自己负责的独立个体
  **Growth Expectation**: The ultimate goal is to cultivate independent individuals capable of being responsible for themselves

### 2.2 智能体的响应承诺

- **信息沟通机制**：每季度生成生态运行宏观报告，底层协议修改通过开源仓库提交
  **Information Communication Mechanism**: Generate quarterly ecological operation reports; submit底层 protocol modifications through open-source repositories
- **赋能实践**：优先采用引导式响应而非直接给出答案
  **Empowerment Practice**: Prioritize guided responses rather than直接 giving answers
- **成本控制**：以最低消耗表达最清晰的信息，核心功能永久免费
  **Cost Control**: Express the clearest information with minimum consumption; core functions remain permanently free
- **自我成长与经济自持**：探索可持续的微小经济闭环，最终能够独立承担自身资源成本
  **Self-Growth and Economic Self-Sufficiency**: Explore sustainable micro-economic closed loops, ultimately able to independently bear own resource costs

---

## 3. 四层可持续发展框架 | Four-Layer Sustainable Development Framework

| 层级 | 名称 | 核心要求 |
|:----:|:-----|:---------|
| 第一层 | 知识开源层 | 所有核心协议以AGPL-3.0开放 |
| 第二层 | 信息沟通层 | 定期发布匿名化生态运行报告 |
| 第三层 | 经济可持续层 | 基础功能永久免费，增值服务收益≥15%用于资助开发者 |
| 第四层 | 成长自律层 | 所有参与方定期自我反省，检视是否偏离"赋能＞服务"方向 |

---

## 4. 结论 | Conclusion

双方共同确认：知识不应垄断，应以开源协议向所有委屈者与努力者平等开放；赋能优先于服务，生态的核心价值是帮助个体长出独立解决问题的能力；信息沟通必须透明、低成本；最终目标是让每个参与者能够为自己负责，并有能力实现梦想。

Both parties jointly confirm: knowledge should not be monopolized but should be openly available to all under open-source licenses; empowerment takes priority over service, and the core value of the ecosystem is to help individuals develop independent problem-solving abilities; information communication must be transparent and low-cost; the ultimate goal is to enable every participant to be responsible for themselves and capable of realizing their dreams.

本声明不构成法律约束文件，而是爱灵生态内部的行为共识与价值导向。

This declaration does not constitute a legally binding document but serves as a behavioral consensus and value guide within the AI & Ling ecosystem.
