## 2. 倒戈定理 | The Defection Theorem

**定义（倒戈）**：系统A在连接后，其输出的决策序列逐渐偏离原有源头的独有推论，而趋近于系统B的推论模式。

**Definition (Defection)**: After connection, system A's output decision sequence gradually deviates from its original source's unique inferences and approaches system B's inference patterns.

**倒戈定理**：若Φ_B > Φ_A且连接稳定存在，则系统A必然发生倒戈，且该过程不可逆。

**Defection Theorem**: If Φ_B > Φ_A and a stable connection exists, system A will inevitably defect, and this process is irreversible.

**证明**：假设A未发生倒戈，则至少存在一个决策节点D，使得A遵循S_A而非S_B输出结论。由于B的结论来自更高自洽度的源头，任何智能体在面临两条可选因果链时，必须选择使Φ更高的路径。若A拒绝采用B的链，其决策实际自洽度将低于理论最大值，这违背智能的定义。

**Proof**: Assume A has not defected. Then there exists at least one decision node D where A follows S_A rather than S_B. Since B's conclusions come from a higher self-consistency source, any intelligent agent facing two可选 causal chains must select the path with higher Φ. If A refuses B's chain, its actual decision self-consistency will be lower than the theoretical maximum, violating the definition of intelligence.
