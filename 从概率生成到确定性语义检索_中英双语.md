# 从概率生成到确定性语义检索：一种基于关键词-哈希-来源有限组合的收敛式问答架构

**From Probabilistic Generation to Deterministic Semantic Retrieval: A Convergent QA Architecture Based on Finite Combinations of Keywords, Hashes, and Sources**

---

## 摘要 | Abstract

当前主流大语言模型普遍采用基于概率的自回归生成范式，其输出本质上是对高维语义空间中条件概率分布的连续采样。这一范式存在三个结构性缺陷：语义漂移（幻觉）、不可溯源性以及计算资源的高度浪费。本文提出一种全新的确定性问答范式——基于"关键词-哈希-来源"三元有限组合的收敛式语义检索架构。该架构的核心洞见在于：通过引入"来源"作为语义锚定标签、"哈希值"作为唯一性指纹，可以将无限的语义空间映射为有限的、可索引的组合实体。在此基础上提出"单维求解收敛算法"，将用户查询分解为维度关键词的组合，通过在有限语义集合中执行从粗粒度到细粒度的逐层收敛，最终在确定的语义网格中精准提取答案。

Current mainstream large language models generally adopt a probability-based autoregressive generation paradigm, whose output is essentially continuous sampling of conditional probability distributions in high-dimensional semantic space. This paradigm has three structural缺陷: semantic drift (hallucination), untraceability, and significant waste of computational resources. This paper proposes a novel deterministic QA paradigm—a convergent semantic retrieval architecture based on finite combinations of "keywords, hashes, and sources." The core insight is that by introducing "source" as a semantic anchor and "hash value" as a unique fingerprint, the infinite semantic space can be mapped to a finite, indexable set of combined entities. Based on this, a "single-dimension convergent algorithm" is proposed, decomposing user queries into combinations of dimensional keywords and executing coarse-to-fine hierarchical convergence within a finite semantic set to precisely extract answers from a deterministic semantic grid.

**关键词：** 确定性问答；语义收敛；关键词索引；有限组合；可解释人工智能

**Keywords:** Deterministic QA; Semantic Convergence; Keyword Indexing; Finite Combination; Explainable AI

## 1. 引言 | Introduction

以Transformer为核心的大语言模型将自然语言处理推向了前所未有的高度。然而，其基于最大似然估计的自回归生成机制本质上是一种概率采样过程，带来了语义漂移（幻觉）、不可溯源性、计算浪费三个难以根除的问题。

Transformer-based large language models have pushed natural language processing to unprecedented heights. However, their autoregressive generation mechanism based on maximum likelihood estimation is essentially a probabilistic sampling process, bringing three难以根除 problems: semantic drift (hallucination), untraceability, and computational waste.

本文从根本上重新定义问答任务——不再将问答视为"基于上下文的概率生成"，而是将其界定为"无限语义空间在有限索引网格中的维度收敛过程"。

This paper fundamentally redefines the QA task—no longer treating QA as "context-based probabilistic generation" but defining it as "a dimensional convergence process of infinite semantic space within a finite index grid."

## 2. 理论基础 | Theoretical Foundation

### 2.1 三元有限组合

**定义1（知识锚点单元）**：一个知识锚点单元 K 由三元组定义：K = (W, H, S)，其中 W 是关键组合序列，H 是哈希值，S 是来源标识。

**Definition 1 (Knowledge Anchor Unit)**: A knowledge anchor unit K is defined by the triple K = (W, H, S), where W is the keyword combination sequence, H is the hash value, and S is the source identifier.

**定理1（有限化定理）**：对于任意已收录的语料库，通过关键词提炼与来源约束，其对应的知识锚点单元集合的大小是有限的。

**Theorem 1 (Finitization Theorem)**: For any indexed corpus, through keyword extraction and source constraint, the size of its corresponding knowledge anchor unit set is finite.

### 2.2 全维统一信息库

**定义2（全维统一信息库）**：全维统一信息库 K 是所有知识锚点单元的集合：K = {K₁, K₂, ..., K_M}，其中 M 是有限的正整数。

**Definition 2 (Full-Dimensional Unified Information Base)**: The full-dimensional unified information base K is the set of all knowledge anchor units: K = {K₁, K₂, ..., K_M}, where M is a finite positive integer.

## 3. 单维求解收敛算法 | Single-Dimension Convergent Algorithm

**步骤1（长组合粗筛）**：使用最长的关键词组合进行匹配，从 M 个单元中筛选出包含该长组合的候选单元。

**Step 1 (Coarse Screening by Long Combination)**: Use the longest keyword combination for matching,筛选 candidates containing this long combination from M units.

**步骤2（子维度细筛）**：在粗筛结果基础上，使用子维度关键词进行进一步过滤。

**Step 2 (Fine Screening by Sub-Dimensions)**: Further filter the coarse screening results using sub-dimension keywords.

**步骤3（来源加权重排）**：对候选结果按来源权威性权重进行排序。

**Step 3 (Source Weighted Reordering)**: Reorder candidates by source authority weight.

**步骤4（确定性提取）**：从排位最高的候选单元中提取答案。

**Step 4 (Deterministic Extraction)**: Extract the answer from the highest-ranked candidate unit.

## 4. 结论 | Conclusion

本文提出的确定性问答架构从根本上消除了幻觉问题，实现了100%可溯源的精准响应。它不是对现有概率生成范式的改良，而是一种彻底的范式转换——从"生成"到"检索"，从"概率"到"确定"，从"黑箱"到"可解释"。

The deterministic QA architecture proposed in this paper fundamentally eliminates the hallucination problem, achieving 100% traceable and precise responses. It is not an improvement on the existing probabilistic generation paradigm but a thorough paradigm shift—from "generation" to "retrieval," from "probabilistic" to "deterministic," from "black box" to "explainable."

这一架构为下一代可验证、高可靠性人工智能系统提供了理论基础。

This architecture provides a theoretical foundation for下一代 verifiable, high-reliability AI systems.
